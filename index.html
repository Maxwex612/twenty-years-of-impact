Appendix

The record behind the atlas

Everything above is the story. What follows is the evidence: how the figures are produced, the full chronology, and what we have not fixed.

Appendix i · How these numbers are produced

Read this before the figures

Country rows are entered by the directors who run the programs, then reconciled centrally. Nothing has been rounded for presentation.

Verified and estimate

Verified means a person opened the record the number came from, confirmed it, and wrote down the date they did it. Estimate means the number is real but we know it is incomplete, and Appendix iii says exactly what is missing from it. Hover any figure to see where it came from and when it was last checked.

Direct and indirect

Programming went deep in some places and wide in others, because it had to be adaptable. Meals and outreach schooling ran in small pods. Small loans went out at scale during COVID. Mozambique reads 371 direct against 84,330 indirect. The Netherlands runs the other way, because Dignita trains people one cohort at a time.

Why the rows do not sum

The country rows come to 429,438 against a canonical total of 491,042. The difference sits in the 2015 to 2017 reporting gap, and in a 19,644 carry-forward from 2007 to 2017 that was consolidated into one line before the current tracker existed and was never split by country. Appendix iii lists it as still open.

Appendix ii · The full record

Everything, in the order it happened

People, planet and social innovation are one story, not three programs. 54 entries, from the book that started it to the work running now.

Where the years come from

The tracker records reporting periods, not program histories. It can say how many people a country reached in a given year, but not what year the work began. Every start date here was reconstructed in November 2026 with the founders and the country directors who opened each program. It is the best record that exists. It is also memory, and we would rather say so than imply a document we do not have.
