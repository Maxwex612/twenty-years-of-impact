How these numbers are produced

Read this before the figures

Country rows are entered by the directors who run the programs, then reconciled centrally. Nothing has been rounded for presentation.

What verified means

A figure marked verified has been checked against its source and the date of that check is recorded. Hover any figure to see its source, what it supersedes, and when a human last confirmed it. A figure marked estimate has a known gap, named at the foot of this page.

Direct and indirect

Programming went deep in some places and wide in others, because it had to be adaptable. Meals and outreach schooling ran in small pods. Small loans went out at scale during COVID. Mozambique reads 371 direct against 84,330 indirect. The Netherlands runs the other way, because Dignita trains people one cohort at a time.

Why the rows do not sum

The country rows come to 427,625 against a canonical total of 491,042. The difference sits in a three-year reporting gap and in historic rows consolidated before the current tracker existed. It is listed as an open gap below.
